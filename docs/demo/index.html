FK steering, played by hand

Interactive companion to the post. Everything here is computed in the browser from the recorded runs: Stable Diffusion 1.5, 100 prompts of the ImageReward benchmark, four particles, seed 2024. No network request beyond the page's own files.

Weights and ESS

Four particles have a guide reward each; the resampler gives particle i the weight exp(λ ri), normalised. The effective sample size, ESS = 1 / Σ wi2, counts how many of the four still matter: move λ, or shift every reward by the same amount, and watch what changes.

Genealogy explorer

One prompt, four particles, five scheduled steps (t = 80 down to 0 of a 100-step DDIM). At each step the guide scores the four current images and the resampler picks parents for the next step: an edge's thickness is the parent's normalised weight, a grey node is a particle nobody copied, and the colour is the starting noise xT that a particle descends from.

Coalescence simulator

Given the weights at each scheduled step, and nothing else about the steering, how many of the k starting noises are expected to survive to the end? The model enumerates every assignment the resampler can produce (the systematic comb over its offset, or the multinomial draw) and propagates the law of the roots exactly (at k = 4; above, a Monte Carlo of 4000 genealogies); the tree below is one realisation.

Reproduction table

Three ways to make an image for the same 100 prompts: one free sample, the best of four free samples, and FK steering at the paper's setting (four particles, λ = 10, systematic resampling at t = 80, 60, 40, 20, 0). The bars are means over prompts with their standard error, seeds averaged first; the line under the table is the difference FK minus best-of-4, computed on the same prompts.

Colours: Okabe-Ito, the same as the figures of the post. Data: the recorded session (probe_C.json) and the baseline runs (sd_baseline.json), exported by scripts/export_demo_data.py. Model code: coalescence.js, a port of collapse_lab/n_coalescence.py.
